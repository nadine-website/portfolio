I am a designer and recent graduate of the RISD, where I studied Graphic Design with a concentration in Computation (because naming things is part of the work). I approach graphic design as both a tool and a way of asking questions through form.

I am currently at SFMOMA and have worked with RISD Design Guild, Yung Studio, Volume 1, and Problem Library (amongst other things). I was named one of GDUSA’s Students to Watch 2026 and received the Joan E. Shewmon Memorial Scholarship in 2025.

I am always happy to talk about potential projects, large or small, speculative or concrete.
